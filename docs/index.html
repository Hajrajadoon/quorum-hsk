The problem

Would you give an LLM your private key?

AI trading agents are everywhere. To use one, you hand it a wallet and hope. One bad output is enough to drain the account.

🌀

Hallucinations

A single model misreads a chart or invents a signal, then trades on it with full confidence.

💉

Prompt injection

A malicious token name, tweet or web page tells the agent to "buy everything" or "send funds".

🔑

Stolen keys

The bot's hot key leaks from a server. Whoever holds it owns everything the bot can touch.

How it works

Debate, guard, execute, prove.

QUORUM wraps an autonomous agent in three layers of trust: no single AI decides, a contract enforces the limits, and every decision leaves a verifiable fingerprint.

Read the market

Live HSKSwap quotes plus a reference price feed. The agent computes EMA trend, RSI, z-score, momentum, volatility regime and the DEX-vs-reference spread for arbitrage.

The council debates

Atlas, Nyx and Sentinel each vote BUY, SELL or HOLD with a confidence and a written rationale. A trade needs a quorum, and Sentinel can veto.

Guardrails check

Size, exposure, slippage, cooldown, buys per day and the daily-loss breaker are checked off-chain, then enforced again by the vault contract itself.

Execute & prove

The trade runs through the vault on HSK Chain. The keccak256 hash of the full debate rides inside the same transaction as Proof of Reasoning.

Meet the council

Three minds, three mandates.

Like a real trading desk, the people who chase profit are separated from the person who manages risk. Each agent runs on an LLM when one is configured, and always on a deterministic quant model, so the system never stops if the AI does.

Risk officer · veto

Protects capital. Approves new risk only when volatility, exposure and the daily loss budget are healthy. Its veto is computed by code, so no LLM can talk it away.

"VETO on new risk: exposure 46.4% is near the 50% cap."
On-chain guardrails

Your rules, enforced by a contract, not a promise.

The QuorumVault contract on HSK Chain holds the funds. The AI's key can only call buy, sell and attest, and every call is checked against the owner's policy. Exits are never blocked, so the agent can always reduce risk.

FAQ

    
Questions, answered.

    
What is QUORUM in one sentence?
An autonomous AI trading agent for HSK Chain where three AI agents must agree before any trade, a vault contract enforces your risk limits, and every decision's reasoning is committed on-chain.

    
Can the AI steal or withdraw my funds?
No. Only the owner's wallet can withdraw. The agent key can only call buy, sell and attest, and only within the policy. If the key leaked, the worst an attacker could do is trade inside your limits, and you can pause the vault with the kill switch.

    
What does "quorum" mean here?
Each decision is put to a vote of three agents. By default 2 of 3 must agree (3 of 3 in conservative mode). The vault contract itself rejects any trade that carries fewer votes than the policy requires.

    
What if the LLM hallucinates or the AI API goes down?
Every agent has a deterministic quant model underneath. An LLM, if configured, reasons on top of the same numbers, and any invalid output falls back to the model. Sentinel's veto is always computed by code, so an LLM can explain it but never lift it.

    
How does Proof of Reasoning work?
The full decision record (votes, rationales, market features, sizing, policy) is serialized to canonical JSON and hashed with keccak256. The hash is emitted on-chain in the trade transaction. Anyone can re-hash the published record and compare. Change one character and the hash no longer matches.

    
Is the stop-loss on-chain?
The daily-loss circuit breaker is fully on-chain: it uses realized PnL and needs no oracle. Stop-loss and take-profit thresholds are stored in the contract and triggered by the agent from live quotes, and sells are never blocked by cooldowns or the breaker.

    
Does it guarantee profit?
No, and no honest trading system can. QUORUM's promise is safe autonomy: whatever strategy runs inside it, it cannot exceed the risk budget the owner set. Strategies are pluggable.

    
Why is there a demo venue in addition to HSKSwap?
Liquidity on many HSKSwap mainnet pairs is still thin, which makes live demos unreliable. The same vault works with the real HSKSwap V3/V2 adapters or with a demo venue for showcases. Switching is a single owner call, setAdapter.

    
Can I control it without coding?
Yes. The dashboard has preset buttons (Conservative, Balanced, Aggressive), a kill switch, and a chat that understands commands like "stop loss 2%", "only trade HSK", "why did you sell?" and "close all".

    
What's next?
HSP agent-to-agent payments, so the council can buy signals from third-party agents per call. Multi-user vault shares (ERC-4626), oracle-based on-chain stop-loss, and a TEE-attested agent runtime.
